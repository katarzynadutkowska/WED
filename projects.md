### Galaxy-in-a-box
<p style="text-align:center"><img src="./Images/gal-in-a-box-shadow.jpeg" alt="galaxy in a box" style="max-width: 65%"></p>
Our database is used in *a galactic model of emission from active star forming regions*, the so-called galaxy-in-a-box model. For more details on this project please visit its GitHub repository (github.com/katarzynadutkowska/galaxy-in-a-box) or publication (Dutkowska & Kristensen 2022, *A&A*, 667, A135 and Dutkowska & Kristensen 2023, *A&A*, 674, A95).
